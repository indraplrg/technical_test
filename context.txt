## Project Requirements

The application is a Student Management System.

There are two tables.

### mahasiswa

- id
- nama
- umur
- nim
- tanggal_lahir
- alamat
- id_jurusan

### jurusan

- id_jurusan
- nama_jurusan
- fakultas
- jenjang

Relationship

One Jurusan has many Mahasiswa.

Every Mahasiswa belongs to one Jurusan.
